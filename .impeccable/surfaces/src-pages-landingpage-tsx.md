# Home page (landing) surface brief

Scope: the public home page, Persuade mode. Replacement world, built first as a sample page outside the repo for the owner's review; the full page follows only on approval.

Audience: someone on her phone after payday, a little embarrassed about her cards, who loves kawaii stationery, cash-stuffing videos and cottagecore. Job: turn a pile of debts into a dated plan, free, no signup. Action: edit the board, see the date, save the plan. Proof: the real payoff math answering live. Constraints: brand fonts (Bagel Fat One, Fredoka, Nunito, Quicksand), Debtsy plus the nine friends, no shame, no timers, no invented claims, existing copy reused.

Memorable moment: the painted debt-free date re-letters itself when a number changes.

Resolved 2026-11-15 (owner): full theme swap, so all nine app themes repaint the whole stand from a paint rack on the counter, and the page loads on Rose Milk (the approved pink).

# Card art provenance

`snowball.webp` and `avalanche.webp` illustrate the two strategy cards in the home page's "Snowball vs Avalanche" section.

- Made with ChatGPT image generation on 2026-11-14, from prompts written for this page, and picked by the site owner.
- Downloaded as 1536x1024 PNGs, resized to 1200x800 WebP (quality 84). Pixels are unedited otherwise; the page frames them and never hue-filters them.
- Each image's exact prompt is embedded next to it in `<name>.webp.json` (Impeccable `embed-prompt` sidecar, since WebP carries no prompt field). Both prompts were second-round redos in one chat, so each sidecar also carries that chat's round-one style block.
- A first round drew strawberries instead of snowballs. It was retired: the style stayed, but strawberries in a snowball-vs-avalanche section would confuse people. Those files are not in the repo.
- ChatGPT added the pink bows and pearl garlands along the top on its own in round one, and the redo kept them.
